# Privacy policy

Housemade is a student project for the course Introduction to Programming at
Nova SBE (2026/27). It is not a commercial service. This page explains what
the app stores about you and why.

## What we store

- **From your Google login:** your name and email address. We use them to
  know who you are and to show your name to your flatmates. We never see or
  store your Google password.
- **What you add in the app:** the flat you create or join, chores, shopping
  items, payments, money in the shared fund, activities and house rules with
  the votes on them. Only the members of your own flat can see this.

## Where it is stored

The data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase in the EU
(Frankfurt). The app itself runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. Your login is
kept in a cookie in your browser, so you stay logged in.

## Weather and activity ideas

- To show the weather, the app sends your IP address to
  [ipinfo.io](https://ipinfo.io) to find your city, and the city's
  coordinates to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com).

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data or share it with anyone, apart from the
  services above.
- We do not use it for advertising or tracking.
- We do not ask Google for anything other than your name and email address.

## Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Elinehogh/Housemade/issues) or ask a member of the
team. The app and its data may be deleted when the course is over.
